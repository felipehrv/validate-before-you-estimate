Classify the following U.S. congressional bill title into exactly one policy topic, using the codebook below. The codebook is condensed from the Comparative Agendas Project master codebook. Choose the single topic that best describes the main purpose of the bill. When a bill touches several topics, code the one that is the primary target of the bill.

CODEBOOK

Health: health care reform; health insurance, Medicare and Medicaid; the drug industry and drug approval; hospitals, clinics and other medical facilities; insurance providers; medical liability; the health care workforce; disease prevention and public health; infant and child health; mental health; long-term care; prescription drug coverage and cost; tobacco; treatment and prevention of drug and alcohol abuse; medical research.

Education: higher education and student loans; elementary and secondary schools; education for underprivileged students; vocational education; special education; educational excellence and standards; education research. Student financial aid belongs here.

Environment: drinking water; waste disposal; hazardous waste and toxic substances; air pollution and climate change; recycling; indoor environmental hazards; protection of species and forests; land and water conservation; environmental research. Energy production, farming, and the management of national parks and public lands do NOT belong here.

Immigration: immigration, naturalization and citizenship; refugees and asylum; visas and the admission of foreign workers and students; enforcement of immigration law.

Law and Crime: law enforcement agencies; white collar and organized crime; illegal drug trafficking and drug law enforcement; courts and court administration; prisons and sentencing; juvenile crime; child abuse; family law and domestic violence; the criminal and civil code; crime control; police. Treatment of drug abuse belongs to Health, not here.

Social Welfare: assistance to low-income families, including food assistance; assistance to the elderly, including Social Security retirement benefits; assistance to people with disabilities; charities and volunteer organizations; child care; general social welfare programs.

Other: everything else, including the economy and taxes, civil rights, agriculture, labor and employment, energy, transportation, housing, commerce, defense and veterans, science and technology, trade, foreign affairs, government operations, commemorative bills, the naming of federal buildings, public lands and parks, arts and culture, and bills for the relief of named individuals.
